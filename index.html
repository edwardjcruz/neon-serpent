HOW TO PLAY

  • Aim: hold and swing the cue stick around the cue ball (mouse: point at the table). ◀ ▶ fine-tune.
  • Shoot: on touch screens press the power strip, pull back and release. With a mouse, set power and press Shoot or Space.
  • Spin: tap the little cue ball — top for follow, bottom for draw, sides for english.
  • Rules: break, then the first ball you pot sets your group (solids or stripes). Pot your whole group, then call a pocket and sink the 8.
  • Fouls give your opponent ball in hand: scratching, hitting nothing, or hitting their ball (or the 8) first. Sinking the 8 early or in the wrong pocket loses the rack.
