Privacy Policy

Neon Serpent · Last updated November 1, 2026

Neon Serpent is a collection of arcade and board games, available as a website and an Android app. There are no accounts and no ads, and we don't sell your data. This page explains what is stored and why.

Stored only on your device

Personal bests, your win and loss records, head-to-head records, sound and difficulty settings, your callsign, and a random player ID. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Sent to our servers if you use online features

  • Global leaderboards: if you choose to submit a score, we store the callsign you typed, the score and the game. These are visible to everyone.
  • Private online rooms: the callsign you typed, the random player ID, the moves in the game and any preset reactions you send. Other players in that room can see your callsign, moves and reactions. Rooms are deleted automatically about 24 hours after they go quiet.
  • Neon Cue ranking: if you play Neon Cue, a rating and game results linked to your random player ID, plus your callsign if it appears on the ranking.
  • Anonymous guest ID: Amazon Cognito gives your device an anonymous ID so it can reach these features. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number.

Single-player and pass-and-play games don't send your gameplay to us.

We don't ask for your real name, email, contacts, photos or location. Please don't use your real name or personal details as a callsign.

Service providers

Online features run on Amazon Web Services, which stores this data on our behalf. As with any internet service, it may process technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver the service. Data is encrypted in transit.

Website analytics

The website, but not the Android app, uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to measure things like which games are played. Google may set cookies and process usage data as described in its privacy policy. The website may also ask permission to show turn alerts; that's optional and can be turned off in your browser.

Keeping and removing data

Leaderboard entries and rankings are kept until removed. To have a callsign or score removed, or to report an offensive callsign, email the address below and tell us the callsign and game. We'll aim to act within 30 days.

Children

Neon Serpent isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted personal details in a callsign, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
